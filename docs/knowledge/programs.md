# Promotion programs

## Promo (daily promotion)
Runs on the same dates for every store, roughly alternating weeks, Monday to Friday only.
Because every store promotes on the same days, its effect is measured within each store over time
(promo days vs comparable non-promo days, controlling for weekday, month, year and holidays).
Pooled causal lift is about +38%; the middle 80% of stores range from about +26% to +56%.
Store type b and "extra" assortment stores respond least.
Caveats: lift is measured against non-promo weeks, which may be depressed by customers shifting
purchases into promo weeks (pull-forward). There is no margin data, so euro figures are revenue,
not profit.

## Promo2 (continuing promotion program)
An opt-in program: each store enrolls on its own start date and then runs promotions renewed in
fixed months (e.g. Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct). Enrolled stores sell less on average, but that is selection:
weaker stores were enrolled. A stacked difference-in-differences comparing newly enrolled stores
with never-enrolled stores estimates about +3% after enrollment, positive in every specification.
However, enrolled stores were declining before enrollment (pre-trend test fails) and a placebo
test is non-zero, so the effect is suggestive, not proven. Recommendation: randomized rollout.

## Promo mechanics pilot (recommended)
The lowest-lift tenth of stores are candidates for an A/B test of an alternative promo mechanic:
randomize half to the new mechanic for two promo cycles and compare against the other half.
